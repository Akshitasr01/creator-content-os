TITLE: Strength training for desk workers
SOURCE_TYPE: notes

- Most clients who sit 8+ hours have weak glutes, tight hip flexors, rounded shoulders
- Don't start with heavy deadlifts. Start with movement quality: glute bridges, bird dogs, dead bugs, wall slides
- 3 sessions per week x 40 minutes is enough for the first 3 months
- Progressive overload: add reps first, then weight. Track every session in a notebook or app
- Walk 7-8k steps daily; sitting less matters as much as training
- Protein: aim for roughly 1.2-1.6 g per kg bodyweight (general guideline, not medical advice)
- Sleep is the most underrated recovery tool - 7+ hours
- Client example: software engineer, 34, back pain from sitting; after 12 weeks of the routine above reported far less stiffness and added 20 kg to his trap-bar deadlift
- Biggest mistake: copying influencer programs designed for people who train 2 hours a day
- Consistency > intensity. A boring program done for 6 months beats a perfect one done for 3 weeks
